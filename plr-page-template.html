What This Is

This is a PLR, Private Label Rights, resource created to help you save time and fast-track your digital product creation process.

Use it as a strong starting point, then make it your own before selling or sharing it.

Customize Before You List

To comply with Etsy guidelines and stand out in the marketplace, do not upload the template exactly as-is. Simple edits can make a big difference.

  • Change the colors and fonts.
  • Swap or remove graphics.
  • Edit the layout or page order.
  • Add your own creative details.
  • Write your own listing description and use your own mockups.

Friendly Reminder

These templates are meant to give you a head start, not replace your creative direction. The more you tailor the design to a specific audience, style, or use case, the stronger your final product will be.
