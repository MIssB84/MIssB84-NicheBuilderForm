Before you start
Bookmark the tool page now so you can come back to it anytime without having to log back in and find it. You will want to use this often.

How to use the tool

1
Enter your niche
Type in a niche you are considering or click one of the quick-fill chips to populate it instantly. It can be broad like "nurse" or more specific like "baseball mom."
2
Add a product type (optional but recommended)
You do not have to enter a product type but it sharpens your results a lot. Type it in or click a chip — PNG, tumbler wrap, shirt, mug, wall art and more are ready to go. Skip it and you will still get results, just broader ones.
3
See what's selling
Hit Find Bestsellers and browse the results on Etsy. Look at what is performing, what styles are showing up, and what gaps you might be able to fill.
